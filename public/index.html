How to use Study Helper

Welcome to your personal AI-powered learning assistant! Here is how to get the most out of the platform:

  • Upload Materials: Upload your PDFs, DOCX, or TXT files. The AI will read them and create study modules based on your syllabus.
  • Chat with your Tutor: Click on any module to ask questions. The AI will explain concepts step-by-step, using your notes as context.
  • Live Classroom: The AI will automatically schedule live classes based on your progress. When the countdown finishes, choose a language and watch the AI draw on the blackboard, use mathematical instruments, and speak the lesson aloud.
  • Exercises & Quizzes: The AI will generate exercises (multiple choice, short answer, or essays) to test your knowledge. You can type your answers or upload a photo of your handwritten work to be graded.
  • Timetable & Vision: The AI creates a personalised study timetable and projects your future exam scores based on your current effort.

About Study Helper

Study Helper is an intelligent, AI-powered educational platform designed to give every student a personal tutor.

Created by Miyoni, this platform combines advanced generative AI with interactive chalkboard drawing, voice narration, and personalized study planning to make learning engaging and accessible.

Whether you are struggling with a complex topic, need a study timetable, or want to test your knowledge, Study Helper adapts to your unique learning style.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Here is how we handle your data:

  • Your Data: All profile information, uploaded study materials, and generated exercises are stored securely to personalize your experience.
  • AI Processing: Your messages and documents are processed by advanced AI models to generate explanations and lessons. We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • Security: We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information and academic data.
